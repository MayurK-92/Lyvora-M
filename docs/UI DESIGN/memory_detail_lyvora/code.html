Crispy Garlic Smashed Potatoes

Prep Time 15m
Servings 4
auto_awesome

AI Synthesis

This technique maximizes the surface area of the potatoes to achieve a deeply crispy exterior while maintaining a fluffy, tender interior. The process involves a three-step method: boiling, smashing, and roasting.

First, small potatoes (like Yukon Gold or New Potatoes) are boiled in heavily salted water until fork-tender. This initial step ensures the starches are fully cooked and ready to crisp up. Once drained and slightly cooled, the potatoes are gently flattened using a heavy object like a glass or measuring cup. The goal is to break the skin and flatten them without completely disintegrating the potato structure.

Finally, the smashed potatoes are generously coated in olive oil infused with garlic and roasted at a high temperature (around 450°F / 230°C). The rough edges created during the smashing process catch the heat and fat, developing an irresistible crunch.

Key Ingredients

Potatoes (Small/Yukon) Garlic Cloves Olive Oil Kosher Salt

Required Tools

kitchen Heavy Baking Sheet cooking Large Pot blender Flat-bottomed Glass

Original Source Snippet

"The secret to the crispiest roasted potatoes isn't just hot oil—it's surface area. By par-boiling and then physically smashing the potatoes before roasting, you create hundreds of tiny nooks and crannies that fry up in the hot fat, yielding a texture that traditional roasting simply cannot match."
